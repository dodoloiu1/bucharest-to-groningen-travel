# Bucharest to Groningen Travel Route & NS Rail Cost Optimizer 🚆✈️

Interactive multi-modal route planner and cost comparison application designed for traveling between **Bucharest Henri Coandă (OTP)** and **Groningen, Netherlands** via low-cost air corridors and the **NS (Nederlandse Spoorwegen)** railway network.

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Direct Booking Buttons for Every Transport Leg:**
  - Direct links to **Wizz Air**, **KLM**, **TAROM**, and **DB**.
  - Direct deep-links to **NS Journey Planner (`ns.nl`)** with exact departure/arrival stations, dates, and times pre-filled.
  - Direct links to airport transfers (Hermes Bus 400/401 on `9292.nl`, Dortmund Airport Express, Flibco).
- **Dedicated Outbound (Jan 28) & Return (Jan 31) Sections:**
  - View individual legs, departure hours, transfer points, and transit durations.
- **Dynamic Cost Calculator:**
  - Adjust luggage options (Underseat, Cabin Trolley, Checked Bag).
  - Select NS fare discount tiers (**NS PrijsTijd Deal** up to -60%, **Standard OVpay / e-Ticket**, or **Dal Voordeel** -40%).
  - Multi-passenger cost distribution.
- **Visual Comparison:**
  - Interactive comparison bar chart comparing total door-to-door round-trip costs across all available routes.

## 🚆 Routes Covered

1. **Via Eindhoven (EIN) &mdash; Lowest Cost:**
   - Outbound: Wizz Air W4 3071 (OTP &rarr; EIN) + Hermes Bus 400/401 + NS Intercity to Groningen.
   - Return: NS Intercity to Eindhoven + Bus 400/401 + Wizz Air back to OTP.
2. **Via Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS) &mdash; Fastest & Zero Train Transfers:**
   - Outbound: KLM / TAROM (OTP &rarr; AMS) + Direct NS Intercity (2h 09m, 0 changes) beneath Schiphol Plaza.
   - Return: Direct NS Intercity (Groningen &rarr; Schiphol) + KLM/TAROM to OTP.
3. **Via Dortmund (DTM):**
   - Outbound: Wizz Air (OTP &rarr; DTM) + Airport Express Coach + DB/Arriva cross-border train.
   - Return: Arriva/DB train + Airport Express + Wizz Air to OTP.
4. **Pure Rail Overland (Zero Flights):**
   - CFR Călători Night Train (Budapest) + ÖBB Railjet + DB ICE + NS to Groningen.
