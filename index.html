💡 Guide to Dutch Rail (NS) Ticketing & Discounts

💳 OVpay Contactless Tapping

No registration or paper tickets needed. Tap your contactless card or Google/Apple Pay phone at the turnstiles when boarding and tap again when exiting. It automatically debits standard 2nd-class fare without paper surcharges.

🏷️ NS PrijsTijd Deals (Up to -60%)

Book on ns.nl or the NS App at least 1 day in advance for off-peak trains. Look for the percentage discount badge. Fares from Schiphol/Eindhoven to Groningen drop from €33.30 down to ~€13.30–€20.00!

🎟️ Dal Voordeel (-40% for 4 People)

The NS Dal Voordeel subscription costs €5.60 for a month (cancel anytime). It gives the cardholder and up to 3 travel companions 40% off during off-peak hours and all weekend. A single round trip saves more than €50 for a group of two!

📱 Official NS App

Download the English "NS" app. It displays live train departure platforms, train length, where to board, and real-time disruption alerts across the whole Netherlands railway network.
